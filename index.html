NASA Missions & Data Sources Citations
Data Source & Grid Resolution Citation: Data sourced from NASA POWER / MERRA-2 reanalysis model (0.5° × 0.625° spatial resolution, covering a ~50–70 km grid cell per point).

TerraSleuth utilizes calibrated multi-decadal Earth observation data from NASA’s open-science repositories:

NASA POWER Project NASA LaRC

Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources. Provides solar, meteorological, and hydrological parameters on a global 0.5° × 0.625° grid natively derived from MERRA-2 and CERES.

URL: https://power.larc.nasa.gov
NASA MERRA-2 GMAO / GSFC

Modern-Era Retrospective analysis for Research and Applications, Version 2. Assimilates space-based observations of atmospheric state, aerosols, and land-surface processes into the GEOS-5 atmospheric model.

NASA GPM (Global Precipitation Measurement) NASA / JAXA

Provides dual-frequency precipitation radar and passive microwave radiometer retrievals incorporated into bias-corrected precipitation reanalysis.

CERES, MODIS, OMI, AIRS & GLDAS NASA Earth Observing System

Ancillary atmospheric composition, surface insolation, vegetation reflectance, and land assimilation modeling used for baseline validation.
